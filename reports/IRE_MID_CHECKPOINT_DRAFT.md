# IRE Mid-Checkpoint Progress Report — Draft

**Team Ceous · updated 25 October 2026 · Draft for the 31 October checkpoint**

## Question and completed evidence

We ask whether approximate-nearest-neighbor (ANN) candidate generation changes *useful supervision and held-out retriever quality* when the selection policy, model, optimisation and **pre-teacher feedback-acquisition budget** are controlled. ANN Recall@200 is agreement with exact top-200 document IDs, not relevance recall. Candidate Jaccard is intersection/union; selected shared fraction is common selected IDs divided by 20.

The **300-update, 50k-passage MS MARCO/ANCE pilot** used known positives and unlabelled mined negatives, **without a teacher**. HNSW/IVF training-query Recall@200 was 0.958155/0.967460; candidate Jaccard was 0.91736 and selected-20 overlap 0.4344. Exact/HNSW/IVF post-training MRR@10 was 0.826063/0.833257/0.850488, all **below untouched ANCE at 0.890777**. Only 2,400 of 20,000 selected negative occurrences per branch entered 300 updates. One seed.

A **separate 100-update MS MARCO pilot** used MiniLM cross-encoder targets, scoring 20 selected negatives plus one known positive per query: 21,000 requested pairs per backend, 32,312 unique across both after caching. Each backend trained on only 800 of 1,000 query lists (16,800 scored-pair exposures). HNSW/IVF MRR@10 was 0.866675/0.855858, again below 0.890777. The teacher assigned approximately 96.4% mean probability to the known positive and picked the same highest-scored document across arms in 993/1,000 queries. **New consumed-query audit:** 30/800 training slates per arm account for **82–83% of summed non-positive teacher target mass**, showing strong concentration, not measured gradient importance. **Neither successful post-training nor feedback efficiency is established.**

## Recovered historical selector falsification — completed 12 October

The **12 October adversarial audit** already recovered the original selector, candidate arrays, qrels and teacher-score records, reproduced all 60,000 saved selected slots across exact/HNSW/IVF, and ran interventions on actual frozen lists. Reordering identical exact top-200 IDs with a one-position cyclic shift reduced selected-20 overlap to **0.09415**; HNSW–IVF overlap under the original rank-position selector was **0.43440**, versus **0.93495** under shared document-ID priorities and **0.98195** for top-20. Of disagreeing selected IDs, **92.39% were available in both candidate pools**. The rank-sensitive coupling explains most *identity disagreement*, **not** the change in final training quality. These are archived findings, not newly run controls.

## Prior work, next steps, and schedule

ANCE uses ANN-mined negatives; RocketQA uses cross-encoder filtering; SimANS studies negative-rank selection. These are close prior mechanisms; no novelty or backend-superiority claim is justified. The 50k MS MARCO diagnostic corpus **deliberately contains all known positives** (535 relevant documents for 500 evaluation queries), so its near-ceiling MRR and relevance recall are not comparable with the full benchmark. Fractional per-query relevance recall is valid with multiple qrels; earlier single-positive integer-lattice concerns do not establish an evaluator error. The separate SciFact pilot has a documented duplicate-query-text risk and a corrected nDCG denominator bug; its results are **not** pooled here.

**Next decisive gate:** The verified 12 October archive is staged in private task scratch, under Codex-owned transfer-index revision 3. A new read-only exact-arm budget audit found that, for the historical 800 training queries, **10,282/16,800 requested teacher pairs are already scored and 6,518 require new scores**; physical cache reuse does not reduce the fixed 21-pair/query acquisition allowance. The original ANCE student initialization now matches the archived parameter hash in an offline CPU check. No exact-arm teacher training has run. **Baseline-path source implementation (25 October):** a new permission-gated zero-update untouched-ANCE evaluator reuses the trained arms' exact 50k re-encoding, top-1000 ranking and qrel functions and passes its metadata-only preflight. No model inference or held-out baseline evaluation was authorized or executed; the historical untouched MRR is therefore still **not** a same-path measured comparator. Before interpreting any trained delta, require a separately authorized, identical-path untouched baseline and inspect discrepancies with archived per-query rankings, including top-k tie handling. Validate a bounded **exact-retrieval teacher positive control** against that baseline; **STOP backend training** if useful learning is not demonstrated. Only then compare exact/HNSW/IVF at matched recall, teacher budget and paired seeds. Pretraining overlap remains an unverified risk. **31 October:** one-page checkpoint. **15 November:** final report and recorded video.
