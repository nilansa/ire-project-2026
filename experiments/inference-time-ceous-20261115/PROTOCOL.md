## Question and competing mechanisms

On the 5,183-document SciFact corpus, does adapting a frozen query vector retrieve judged-relevant documents missed by the original 50-document pool, beyond promotion within that pool? A query vector and document vector each have 384 real coordinates; their dot product is their retrieval score. Query-specific updates do not change the frozen encoder or document vectors.

| Mechanism | Prediction and alternative | Comparator and falsification screen |
|---|---|---|
| H1: teacher-directed query movement | Teacher-guided movement discovers relevant documents outside the original 50; alternatively the benefit is only in-pool reranking or extra retrieval. | Compare the same moved query over the full corpus and only the original pool; compare unmodified dense, deeper 100, unweighted Rocchio, weighted Rocchio, direct reranking, and TOUR. Park if the best development choice fails the threshold below or adds no outside-pool relevant hits. |
| H2: local covariance changes direction | A regularized inverse covariance direction improves the query update at the same complete Euclidean displacement after decay; alternatively isotropic movement is as effective. | Same initial pool, teacher, temperature, gradient, step size, decay and full-corpus retrieval as H1. Reject the proposed direction benefit at this screen if H2 fails the threshold against its matched TOUR comparator. |
| Feedback ranking without movement | Scoring the initial top 20 improves ordering but cannot discover a document outside that set; alternatively quality gains require full-corpus discovery. | Direct teacher reranking of 20 and the original-pool-only moved-query counterparts. Treat the 50-score rerank as a higher-cost diagnostic. |

These are different causal families: feedback gradients, local feature geometry, and fixed-pool sorting. A neighboring-field analogy for H2 is covariance preconditioning in optimization; it does not add representational expressivity: transforming the document in a bilinear score is equivalent to transforming the query. A paired development gain of at least **0.01 absolute nDCG@10** over the strongest applicable baseline at no greater teacher-pair and scan budgets is the provisional practical threshold; also inspect the corresponding retrieval-slot and latency costs. H2's primary comparator is TOUR with the same teacher and step, and any difference in selected step is a separate tuning comparison. If neither H1 nor H2 meets the screen, PARK; do not promote weak or ambiguous findings. This is a screening rule, not an inferential significance test. An independent reviewer must judge mechanism distinction and decision thresholds before execution.

## Frozen methods and costs

The initial exact search returns 50 documents. The final ranked outputs use the first 10 and 20 positions. Scores use raw inner products, stable document-key ties, and the same corpus for all methods. All feedback comes from the initial pool; no label is available to an update. The primary teacher is MedCPT, revision `71caf65d4927987813984f54c284405a13fcca49`; BGE, revision `953dc6f6f85a1b2dbfca4c34a2796e7dde08d41e`, is a separately reported sensitivity arm. Both use the historical 50-pair cache; direct application of a teacher arm logically costs 20 query-document scores, except rerank-50 costs 50. Fresh physical teacher calls here are zero; historical creation required 50 scores per query per teacher and is separate overhead.

* Dense: original exact top 50; deeper-100 reference is one scan and 100 returned slots.
* Unweighted Rocchio: add a fixed multiple of the mean of the first five document vectors to the query. The fixed multiples are 0.05, 0.2, 0.5 and 1.0.
* Teacher-weighted Rocchio: add the weighted mean of the initial 20 document vectors, with weights given by softmax of cached raw teacher scores divided by a temperature of either 0.5 or 4.0. Use the same four multipliers.
* TOUR-soft transferred baseline: compute the teacher probability over the 20 initial feedback documents using each of those two temperatures. Compute the student probability using their raw query-document dot products and temperature 1. The feedback direction is the weighted sum of those 20 document vectors using teacher probabilities minus the weighted sum using student probabilities. Add step size times this direction to the query and separately subtract step size times 0.01 times the original query. Step sizes are 0.05, 0.2, 0.8 and 3.2. Also test the paper's stopping rule: if the dense first result has a maximum teacher score among the 20, do not update or perform the second search. Ties count as a maximum. This is a paper-derived, one-step, single-vector transfer, not an official-code reproduction or full multi-round TOUR. The paper reports SGD momentum 0.99, weight decay 0.01, and one DPR iteration with learning rate 0.2 and top-100 retrieval/reranking; a single first step has no momentum history, and our 20-feedback pool differs. See equations 12–13, section 3.5, and appendix D in [Sung et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.354.pdf).
* H2: center the 50 original document vectors and form their uniformly weighted population covariance; this is local covariance, not an exact Fisher matrix. Its mean diagonal variance sets the scale. For each shrinkage multiplier 0.1, 1 or 10, apply the inverse of covariance plus multiplier times mean variance times the identity to the same TOUR feedback direction. First normalize that direction to the original feedback gradient's length, then form the entire update including the same separate decay. Finally rescale this complete update to the Euclidean length of the corresponding unpreconditioned TOUR update, including decay, at the same teacher, temperature, step and stopping setting. H2 uses the non-stopping setting; its matched TOUR comparator also does not stop. The inverse uses an economy singular-value decomposition including the orthogonal complement. A zero target step produces a zero update; zero mean variance or a zero H2 step with nonzero target blocks execution. The full-precision norms must match to relative tolerance 1e-12; after float32 query conversion, their difference must be at most eight float32 machine epsilons times the larger of the query norm and matched step norm. Record applied and matched step lengths, gradient angle and complete-step angle. The identity comparator is the TOUR branch itself.
* Rerank-20 is the same-slate paid-feedback comparator: it sorts only the initial top 20 by cached teacher scores and returns at most 20, with 20 logical teacher pairs and no fresh calls. Rerank-50 sorts only the initial 50 and costs 50 logical teacher pairs. Neither adds unseen documents. For each moved-query configuration, a separately timed `:pool` arm sorts only the original 50 using the moved query, without another corpus scan; this counterpart isolates promotion.

A non-stopped, full-corpus adapted method has two logical full-corpus scans, 100 returned slots and at most 100 distinct returned documents; report the actual union, including overlap. Dense, rerank, a stopped update and a `:pool` arm have one scan and 50 returned slots. The deeper reference has one scan and 100 returned slots. Dense requests 50 candidates; the deeper reference requests 100. Exact top-k uses a partition followed by a deterministic sort of all candidates at or above the boundary, so a tied boundary can require sorting more than the requested number. These are not equal-compute conditions. Per query and arm, physical harness work includes one untimed warmup plus three timed passes, and is recorded separately: exact-search calls and returned slots, sort calls and candidate counts, and one teacher-cache row lookup with its distinct score values. The manifest separately reports total cache row lookups, the total values in those views and the count of distinct teacher/query/document triples across all arms; per-arm deployment pair budgets must not be summed as though overlapping cached pairs were distinct. The three passes each repeat the exact CPU search and update; they exclude cache lookup, encoder and teacher inference. These are not ANN, teacher-latency or end-to-end measurements. The one configured thread, software and hardware are recorded. No fresh teacher inference, model training, remote GPU, paid service or API call is authorized in this subtask.

The fixed development grid has 206 arm configurations: 2 dense/deep references, 4 direct reranks, 4 unweighted Rocchio configurations, and 96 teacher-dependent movement configurations (two teachers times two temperatures times 24 settings: 4 weighted Rocchio, 8 TOUR and 12 H2), plus 100 pool-only counterparts. This is 106 full/reference arms and 100 pool arms. Teacher entropy and maximum probability over the first 20 scores are recorded for each teacher at both temperatures; lower entropy and a maximum near one indicate saturation. These are diagnostics, not judgment-based quality metrics.

## Selection, scoring and controls

Freeze protocol, code, source and duplicate-safe split hashes before any metric. Predictions and manifests are written to new unique output directories and hashed before scoring. Development is randomly ordered with seed 20261115. Score every fixed development configuration and retain all raw per-query metrics. Choose the largest mean development nDCG@10 independently per method/teacher family, including temperature as a development-only choice; teachers are never selected against one another on INNER. Exact ties prefer fewer average full scans, then the smaller configured step, then lexical arm name. Keep every family winner for INNER even if it loses to dense. Record dense separately as the deployment fallback whenever that winner does not strictly exceed dense. Once frozen, INNER predictions run only for those family winners, their pool counterparts, matched TOUR comparators and fixed references. INNER metrics must never choose or revise settings, temperature, teacher, stopping, claims or thresholds.

The score stage alone loads all train qrels for each query, including relevant documents outside every returned slate. Compute nDCG@10 with the ideal ranking over all positive judgments, MRR@10, recall@10/20, original-pool recall@50, original-pool oracle nDCG@10 and recall@10/20, gain outside original 50 at 10/20, relevant documents lost relative to the original top 10/20, entries outside the original pool at 10/20, union recall over all returned slots, and judged fraction at 10. Also record the teacher's highest-scoring feedback document's presence in the final 20 as a separate teacher-agreement diagnostic. Unjudged documents are not established negatives; reported gains and ceilings are judgment-relative.

For each query, partition judged-relevant documents into the original top-20 feedback set F20, the rest of the original top-50 set U50 minus F20, and documents outside U50. Report the total relevant documents in each region and, at final ranks 10 and 20, the count present and distinct gains and losses against the dense top-10 or top-20 set. This separates within-feedback promotion, within-pool promotion and genuine outside-pool discovery. F20 is a fixed analysis region even for rerank-50, whose actual feedback includes the other 30 documents. Report per-query initial-pool judged counts and fraction, initial-pool positive counts and zero-positive indicator, plus qrels-missing and no-positive-qrels indicators. Aggregate both means and counts. If qrels are absent, judgment-dependent counts and indicators are null rather than treated as zero; selection is also blocked. Zero-positive qrels likewise leave ranking relevance metrics undefined and block selection, rather than silently changing the evaluation population.

Per-query CSV and JSONL, summary, costs and full provenance are saved. Use 2,000 paired query bootstrap draws with seed 20261115 for exploratory nDCG@10 differences against dense; do not claim corrected significance across multiple arms. The few same-text queries remaining within a partition are dependent: query-level intervals do not account for group clustering and should not support a confirmatory claim. Independent review must address this limitation before publication.

Synthetic checks must pass before any metric run: the original all-qrels denominator, planted discovery versus fixed-pool promotion, and label-invariant stable ties; additionally verify noncollinear H2 complete-step norm equality, losing-family retention, the three-region decomposition and missing-qrels handling, exact top-50 versus top-100 requests, and temperature diagnostics. Alignment is a separate gate. The planted case is a positive control; label permutation and forbidden outside-pool entry are distinct negative checks. A changed source, missing file, invalid score, failed mapping, leakage, failed synthetic control, or runtime beyond 1,800 seconds invalidates and stops the run. Preserve incomplete runs; use a fresh unique directory for any corrected rerun. Independent scientific review, a recorded owner/reservation and root's committed protocol remain mandatory.

H5, alternate feedback selection from stratified ranks, is a future separate protocol and is not implemented or combined with H2. No old P0/P0-prime, synthetic history, HotpotQA, MuSiQue or DISCo experiment is repeated.
